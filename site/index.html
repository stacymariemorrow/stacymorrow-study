About

Stacy Morrow

I am a communications strategist with nearly twenty years across music, media, and the internet, now a graduate student at the University of Denver in Media and Globalization with a certificate in Public Diplomacy. The long goal is a PhD in mass communication and AI.

Before the program I ran media campaigns for brands including Disney, Hallmark, and Uber, built audiences for artists and venues, and ran Alice in Amsterdam, a media experiment that reached more than 130,000 views across 53 countries. I started in music: street teams, booking, college radio, and harm reduction work with DanceSafe in Denver.

The question under all of it: who owns media, how it sorts people, and what that sorting does to them. From Tulsa, Oklahoma. Based in Denver, Colorado.
